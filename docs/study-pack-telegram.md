━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⚠️ *XAU/USD (GOLD) — HONEST WARNING*
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Gold is one of the HARDER assets for a beginner. Reasons:

  • Wide intraday range (200–500 pips/day)
  • Heavily news-driven (NFP, CPI, FOMC, DXY, real yields)
  • Round numbers (2400, 2450, 2500) attract liquidity grabs
  • Spreads widen on news + Mondays

*My suggestion:* Practice on EUR/USD OTC first (available 24/7 on IQ Option, doesn't gap on weekends). Build 50+ paper trades there, then graduate to XAU/USD 4h.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📋 *WHAT TO PRACTICE WHILE WATCHING*
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

After each batch of videos:

  □ Open IQ Option demo
  □ Pick EUR/USD OTC, 5-min candles
  □ Identify 3 support/resistance levels on your own
  □ Wait for price to test a level
  □ Note entry, SL, TP — don't actually trade yet
  □ After 4h, check what price did

Goal: 30 paper setups logged *before* you place a single real trade.
